What does it take to reason about time? 🧮 Working with dates and clocks, or 🧩 understanding event order and cause and effect? Our new [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29507) tests that split through cross-benchmark transfer experiments and cognitive-demand analysis.
